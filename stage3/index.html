KPI Card

Drag two fields onto the shelves on the Marks card and this becomes a live KPI card.

  • Measure A continuous measure — the number to total. e.g. Sales, Profit, Orders
  • Date A date field — the timeline the card is cut by. e.g. Order Date
Once both are on, the card shows:
  • The measure totalled for the latest period in the data, as one big number.
  • Its change against the period before — or the same period a year ago.
  • A trend chart across the calendar year, bucketed at the same grain, with last year behind it.
  • Optional goal tracking: a % to goal readout and a solid status bar.

The gear in the top right opens settings — period grain, number format, goal, palette, font and layout. Everything you choose is saved with the workbook.

The grain drives the whole card — the headline total, the comparison, and the buckets in the trend chart.

Number format

Off by default so the card can be checked against Tableau exactly. With it on, hover the big number to see the full figure.

Trend chart

The chart always spans the calendar year, bucketed at the grain above.

Goal tracking

The bar is a solid status colour, never a progress fill. The proportion is spelled out in the “% to goal” line under the number.

Layout & appearance

Palette and font preview straight away, so you can judge them against the live card before saving.
